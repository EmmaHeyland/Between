Between

Clickable prototype · MOD09 Innovation & Entrepreneurship · Group 2

Demo flow for the pitch

  1. Book a haircut from Daan. Your payment is held, not sent.
  2. Open Bookings, confirm Yusuf's bike repair and leave a review.
  3. Go to Earn and switch on exam week mode. Your services disappear from Explore.
  4. Add a new service of your own and find it in Explore.

Yellow buttons marked "Demo" simulate the other person's action, so you can show the full escrow cycle on your own.

All people, prices and reviews are example data.
